How to ride safely on roads shared with motor vehicles.

🪖 1. Always Wear a Helmet

Your helmet is your most important safety gear. Ensure it fits snugly and the strap is buckled before you start riding.

🚗 2. Ride WITH Traffic

Always ride on the left side of the road, moving in the same direction as cars. Never ride against traffic.

✋ 3. Use Hand Signals

Drivers can't read your mind. Always signal your turns and stops clearly using your arms (see diagram below).

🚦 4. Obey Traffic Lights

Bicycles are vehicles too. Stop at red lights and stop signs. Never run a red light, even if the road looks empty.

🚪 5. Watch for Car Doors

When riding past parked cars, leave at least 1 meter of space. A driver might suddenly open their door into your path.

🎧 6. No Headphones

Keep your ears open. You need to hear cars approaching, horns honking, or other cyclists warning you of danger.

✋ Essential Hand Signals

Extend your arm clearly so drivers behind you can see it.

👈

Left Turn

Extend your left arm straight out.

👆

Right Turn

Extend your left arm out and bend your elbow upward (L-shape).

👇

Stopping

Extend your left arm out and bend your elbow downward.
